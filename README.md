# Rocket

This repository contains all the 3D printable files, flight tips, and documentation for the model rockets I’ve designed, printed, and launched.

## Rocket Models

| Version | Body | 3D Printed Parts | Compatible Motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | 3D printed | Full rocket body and parts | A6-3 |
| **V2** | 3D printed | Full rocket body and parts | A6-3, B6-2 |
| **V3** | 25 cm carbon fiber tube | Nose cone, upper fin section, lower fin section | A6-3, B6-4 |

## Rocket V3

V3 is the latest version and is a major redesign compared with V1 and V2.

The biggest change is that V3 no longer uses a fully 3D-printed main body. Instead, it uses a **25 cm long carbon fiber tube**, making the design lighter and stronger.

### Required Carbon Fiber Tube

V3 requires a carbon fiber tube with these dimensions:

- **Length:** 25 cm
- **Outer diameter:** 27 mm
- **Inner diameter:** 25 mm

### 3D Printed V3 Parts

V3 only requires **3 main 3D-printed pieces**:

- **Nose cone**
- **Upper fin section**
- **Lower fin section**

All required V3 files can be found inside the **V3 folder** in this repository.

### V3 Compatible Motors

V3 is designed to work with:

- **A6-3**
- **B6-4**

## Launch Setup

The launch stand is a simple tripod design with a vertical launch rod.

I use a standard wired launch controller. The igniter is connected to the motor following normal model rocket safety procedures.

## 3D Printing Instructions

### Recommended Material

- **Bambu Lab PLA Aero** is highly recommended.

### Print Settings

- Use standard PLA settings
- **3 walls**
- **10–16% infill**
- Sand all rough edges after printing for a smoother finish and better aerodynamics

### V3 Printing

For V3, only these parts need to be printed:

- Nose cone
- Upper fin section
- Lower fin section

The main rocket body is the **25 cm carbon fiber tube**.

## Flight Tips

For more stable and higher flights:

- Add **launch lugs** or rail buttons near the bottom of the rocket body
- Make sure the fins are properly aligned
- Make sure the rocket has enough nose weight for stability
- Check that all parts are securely attached before launch

### Personal Best

My personal best altitude so far is approximately:

**~100 meters**

## Recovery System

- **Main parachute:** 100 cm wide
- The parachute is attached to the nose cone with a nylon cord

### Packing Method

1. Roll the parachute tightly
2. Fold it in half
3. Insert it into the rocket body

## Safety

Always follow local model rocket safety guidelines and the instructions provided by the rocket motor manufacturer.

Launch only in suitable open areas away from:

- People
- Buildings
- Trees
- Power lines
- Dry vegetation
- Other hazards

Always check the rocket, motor, recovery system, and launch equipment before every flight.
